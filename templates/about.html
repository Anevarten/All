Who We Are

Committed to natural sleep since day one

Our Story

Natures Slumber was born from a simple belief: that the materials we sleep on matter as much as the air we breathe. Nestled in Chennai, we set out to bring 100% certified organic latex products to homes across India.

Every mattress, every pillow, and every topper we make is traceable back to sustainably managed rubber plantations. We hold ourselves to the highest global standards — GOLS, GOTS, OEKO-TEX, and USDA Organic — so you don't have to take our word for it.

Sustainability

Rubber trees absorb CO₂ as they grow — our products are carbon negative from harvest to your home.

Transparency

Every certification is verifiable. We don't hide behind marketing — we let the science speak.

Community

We partner with farmers and artisans who share our commitment to ethical, sustainable practice.
